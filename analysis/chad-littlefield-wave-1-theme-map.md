# Chad Littlefield Wave 1 Theme Map

If this theme map is being used to drive learner-facing extraction, pair it

## Main themes

### 1. Curiosity beats default conversation scripts

Representative sessions:

- `#23 3 Conversation Starters That Work Better Than Icebreakers`
- `#142 How To Gently Eradicate Small Talk`
- `#341 How To Go From Small Talk To Deep Conversation`
- `#408 Tips & Tools For Better Small Talk`

What this theme appears to govern:

- upgrading beyond canned small talk
- choosing more revealing or energizing prompts
- getting out of autopilot social scripts

### 2. Question design shapes trust and honesty

Representative sessions:

- `#46 The Real Power of a Question (Beyond Icebreakers)`
- `#185 Ask Powerful Questions To Create Conversations That Matter`
- `#347 How To Ask Questions To Get The Answers You Want`
- `#423 How To Start A Conversation In A Business Meeting`

What this theme appears to govern:

- psychological safety
- exploratory versus leading questions
- more honest answers
- better work and group conversations

### 3. Reflection can move a conversation better than another question

Representative sessions:

- `#351 How To Have A Conversation Without Asking Questions`
- `#337 How To Bring Up Topics In A Conversation`
- `#86 Ways to Start a Conversation [UNCUT Interview with @colejennette]`

What this theme appears to govern:

- verbatim reflection
- translation and paraphrase
- reflecting unstated feelings
- connecting dots across a conversation

### 4. Awkwardness is editable through naming, attention, and redirection

Representative sessions:

- `#369 How To Not Be Awkward In A Conversation`
- `#362 How To Get Out Of An Awkward Conversation`
- `#397 How To Change The Topic Of Conversation`
- `#402 The Notecard Method: How Not To Be BORING In A Conversation`

What this theme appears to govern:

- recovering from weird moments
- reducing self-consciousness
- changing topics cleanly
- exiting or redirecting without collapse

### 5. Good conversation mechanics transfer into meetings and group rooms

Representative sessions:

- `#423 How To Start A Conversation In A Business Meeting`
- `#297 How To Start A Conversation In A Group, Virtually And In-Person`
- `#294 How To Keep Someone Interested In A Training Or Conversation`
- `#273 How To Deal With People Who Dominate Conversation`

What this theme appears to govern:

- meeting openings
- group entry
- inclusion and engagement
- managing airtime and attention

### 6. Humor works as shared release and surprising turn

Representative sessions:

- `#210 How To Create Humor In Conversations`
- `#397 How To Change The Topic Of Conversation`
- `#86 Ways to Start a Conversation [UNCUT Interview with @colejennette]`

What this theme appears to govern:

- laughter as tension release
- humor through surprise or trajectory shift
- energy making lines land better
- connection rather than domination
