# Cross-Corpus Concept Backlog

Merged concept backlog across the first three transcript-backed speaking
corpora.

Date: August 10, 2026.

## Purpose

This file identifies the concepts that recur strongly enough across
`Ultraspeaking`, `Vinh Giang`, `Think Fast Talk Smart`, and now `Charisma on
Command` to justify durable concept pages later.

If you want the concept backlog to stay tied to learner-usable behavior, pair
it with `analysis/transcript-backed-concrete-speaking-approaches.md` so each
concept family has a clear move-level bridge.

## Tier 1: Clear cross-corpus concepts

### Confidence as visible behavior

Why it belongs:

- `Ultraspeaking` frames confidence as composure and staying in character.
- `Vinh Giang` frames confidence as trained response and visible command.
- `Think Fast Talk Smart` frames confidence as composure, credible structure, and calm presence.

Likely future concept page:

- `concepts/confidence-as-visible-behavior.md`

Best exact approaches:

- `Approach 3: Pause, Frame, Then Answer`
- `Approach 15: Rehearse The Pressure Shape, Not Just The Content`

### Speaking under pressure

Why it belongs:

- `Ultraspeaking` covers blanking, recovery, and anti-anxiety reframing.
- `Vinh Giang` strongly covers freezing, rambling, and question pressure.
- `Think Fast Talk Smart` covers first moments, answering well, and composed openings.

Likely future concept page:

- `concepts/speaking-under-pressure.md`

Best exact approaches:

- `Approach 2: Replace Fillers With Deliberate Silence`
- `Approach 3: Pause, Frame, Then Answer`

### Clarity over perfection

Why it belongs:

- `Ultraspeaking` links clarity to delivery, not only script precision.
- `Vinh Giang` ties clarity to articulation and removing clutter.
- `Think Fast Talk Smart` explicitly prioritizes understandability over perfect polish.

Likely future concept page:

- `concepts/clarity-over-perfection.md`

Best exact approaches:

- `Approach 14: Simplify Complex Ideas By Layering`
- `Approach 23: Improve Intelligibility Without Accent Erasure`

### Audience-first communication

Why it belongs:

- `Ultraspeaking` reaches it through listener attention and emotional engagement.
- `Vinh Giang` reaches it through conversational control and first impression.
- `Think Fast Talk Smart` states it explicitly through goal, audience, and message design.

Likely future concept page:

- `concepts/audience-first-communication.md`

Best exact approaches:

- `Approach 5: Prepare With Goal, Audience, And Constraints`
- `Approach 14: Simplify Complex Ideas By Layering`

### Pause as a strength signal

Why it belongs:

- `Ultraspeaking` treats pause as composure.
- `Vinh Giang` treats pause as the replacement for fillers and rambling.
- `Think Fast Talk Smart` uses calm openings and structured responses that benefit from pause discipline.

Likely future concept page:

- `concepts/pause-as-strength.md`

Best exact approaches:

- `Approach 2: Replace Fillers With Deliberate Silence`

## Newly justified by the Charisma corpus

The `Charisma on Command` wave materially upgraded a few concept families from
promising to durable:

### Self-respect over approval-seeking

Why it now belongs:

- `Charisma on Command` makes the pattern explicit through confidence and
  respect material.
- `Vinh Giang` supports it through low-status habit analysis and the
  likability-versus-respect tension.
- `Ultraspeaking` supports it through express mode over impress mode.
- `Think Fast Talk Smart` supports it through calm credibility and less
  impression management.

Likely concept page:

- `concepts/self-respect-over-approval-seeking.md`

Best exact approaches:

- `Approach 7: Remove Low-Status Leakage`
- `Approach 12: Build Respect Through Steadiness Instead Of Proving`

### Curiosity and conversational depth

Why it now belongs:

- `Think Fast Talk Smart` explicitly argues that curiosity beats charisma.
- `Charisma on Command` adds substantial material on appreciation, deeper
  conversation, and awkwardness recovery.
- `Vinh Giang` supports it through better question patterns and conversation
  navigation.
- `Ultraspeaking` supports it through small-talk ease and audience connection.

Likely concept page:

- `concepts/curiosity-and-conversational-depth.md`

Best exact approaches:

- `Approach 8: Use Curiosity To Create Better Conversations`
- `Approach 16: Start With Connection Before Content`

### Storytelling as lived experience

Why it now belongs:

- `Ultraspeaking` explicitly frames better storytelling as reducing abstraction
  and bringing the audience into a specific lived moment.
- `Vinh Giang` links story directly to openings, connection, and audience
  investment rather than treating it as a separate performance skill.
- `Think Fast Talk Smart` frames story as a learnable communication tool built
  through detail, emotion, sequencing, and deliberate study.

Likely concept page:

- `concepts/storytelling-as-lived-experience.md`

Best exact approaches:

- `Approach 18: Build The Story Around One Embodied Moment`

### Disagreement without domination

Why it now belongs:

- `Think Fast Talk Smart` explicitly reframes negotiation and argument away
  from battle and toward relationship management, inquiry, and learning.
- `Vinh Giang` adds practical frameworks for high-stakes conversations where
  you need both a clear position and a non-combative structure.
- `Charisma on Command` supports the pattern through respect and frame control
  that reject brittle domination.

Likely concept page:

- `concepts/disagreement-without-domination.md`

Best exact approaches:

- `Approach 3: Pause, Frame, Then Answer`
- `Approach 19: Take A Position, Give A Reason, Then Reopen`

### Warmth versus authority

Why it now belongs:

- `Vinh Giang` explicitly frames charisma as warmth plus competence and then
  shows how small cues affect respect.
- `Think Fast Talk Smart` explicitly argues that people often need warmth
  before competence can fully land.
- `Charisma on Command` supports the balance through respect, steadiness, and
  visible appreciation without submissiveness.

Likely concept page:

- `concepts/warmth-versus-authority.md`

Best exact approaches:

- `Approach 16: Start With Connection Before Content`
- `Approach 20: Lead With Warmth So Competence Can Land`

### Executive presence

Why it now belongs:

- `Ultraspeaking` explicitly frames executive presence as grounded ease and
  unhurriedness under pressure rather than strain or over-control.
- `Vinh Giang` supports it through professional credibility, vocal image,
  first impressions, and status-signal cleanup.
- `Think Fast Talk Smart` supports it through composed presence, body and
  voice calibration, and authentic professional communication.
- `Charisma on Command` supports it through respect, steadiness, and frame
  control rather than brittle status theater.

Likely concept page:

- `concepts/executive-presence.md`

Best exact approaches:

- `Approach 7: Remove Low-Status Leakage`
- `Approach 21: Use Unhurried Pace To Claim Space`

### Quick-wittedness

Why it now belongs:

- `Vinh Giang` directly argues that wit is trainable through foundations,
  presence, and deliberate review after missed opportunities.
- `Think Fast Talk Smart` supports it by reframing humor as collaborative
  jamming rather than self-centered joking.
- `Charisma on Command` supports it through frame-breaking, playful noticing,
  and making flat interaction more alive.
- `Ultraspeaking` supports it through reduced withdrawal, easier associations,
  and greater permission to say the funny or interesting thing.

Likely concept page:

- `concepts/quick-wittedness.md`

Best exact approaches:

- `Approach 11: Use Humor As Shared Relief, Not Performance`
- `Approach 17: Put The Turn Late And Leave Room For The Laugh`

### Persuasion and influence

Why it now belongs:

- `Think Fast Talk Smart` explicitly reframes negotiation as any attempt to
  persuade or influence and adds strong pitch and argument material.
- `Vinh Giang` explicitly argues that influence improves when you start with
  what the other person wants and reduce self-centered framing.
- `Charisma on Command` supports influence through frame leadership, trust, and
  visible social courage rather than slickness.

Likely concept page:

- `concepts/persuasion-and-influence.md`

### Accent realism and multilingual communication

Why it now belongs:

- `Think Fast Talk Smart` makes the strongest direct case that accent is not a
  defect, clarity matters more than conformity, and intelligibility can improve
  without accent erasure.
- `Vinh Giang` supports the concept through articulation, explanation design,
  and professional clarity work that improve understanding without requiring
  identity suppression.
- `Ultraspeaking` supports the pressure side through musicality, ease, and
  visible confidence that help multilingual speakers stay understandable under
  attention.

Likely concept page:

- `concepts/accent-realism-and-multilingual-communication.md`

### Hard-question handling

Why it now belongs:

- `Ultraspeaking` contributes the visible-composure and acknowledgment layer
  through difficult-question breakdowns.
- `Vinh Giang` gives the clearest direct training loop for pausing, framing an
  answer, asking for time, and staying useful under pressure.
- `Think Fast Talk Smart` adds paraphrasing, bottom-line thinking, and
  structure-first answering under uncertainty.

Likely concept page:

- `concepts/hard-question-handling.md`

### Founder-style and business communication

Why it now belongs:

- `Think Fast Talk Smart` contributes the strongest explicit pitch and
  communication-planning models for goal, audience, value, and next step.
- `Vinh Giang` adds the practical professional layer: remove softeners, answer
  the real question, and stop overwhelming the listener with unnecessary
  context.
- `Ultraspeaking` adds the expressive leadership layer so updates and business
  messages communicate what matters instead of sounding dead on arrival.

Likely concept page:

- `concepts/founder-style-and-business-communication.md`

### Investor-style and objection-heavy communication

Why it now belongs:

- `Think Fast Talk Smart` contributes the clearest direct model for skeptical
  rooms through pitch, negotiation, and anti-win-the-argument communication.
- `Vinh Giang` contributes the strongest pre-game mapping pattern for
  high-stakes conversations where friction and resistance are likely.
- `Ultraspeaking` contributes the visible-composure layer needed when the room
  is testing, accusing, or evaluating rather than simply listening.

Likely concept page:

- `concepts/investor-style-and-objection-heavy-communication.md`
