# Dry Bar Comedy Wave 2 Starter Drill Candidates

Transfer hypotheses from a multi-performer humor-exemplar slice. These are not
validated effectiveness interventions.

## 1. Premise-to-promise opening

1. Choose one presentation topic.
2. State the specific tension or question the audience will see.
3. Deliver the promised example within the first minute.
4. Ask a listener whether the opening promise was accurate and useful.

Source anchors: `#2`, `#6`, `#18`.

## 2. Concrete-friction inventory

1. List five small, observable frictions related to your topic.
2. Choose one with a person, object, place, and changed condition.
3. Explain the general lesson only after describing the event.
4. Remove details that do not change the listener's understanding.

Source anchors: `#1`, `#2`, `#39`.

## 3. Three-frame contrast

1. Write the expected frame, the observed complication, and the more accurate
   frame.
2. Say all three without mocking the first frame.
3. Add one boundary showing when the revised frame does not apply.
4. Ask a listener to restate what changed.

Source anchors: `#12`, `#15`, `#30`.

## 4. Heckle/recovery transfer

1. Ask a partner to interrupt a two-minute explanation with a relevant concern.
2. Acknowledge the concern in one sentence.
3. Answer or redirect without abandoning the main thread.
4. Review whether the repair preserved dignity and clarity.

Source anchor: `#24`; validate against the existing repair protocol.

## 5. Humor-risk transfer filter

1. Write the humorous observation you want to use.
2. Identify who bears the cost if the line fails.
3. Remove material that distracts, divides, or disparages.
4. Keep the line only if it clarifies the topic and survives a non-performance
   setting check.

Source bridge: Dry Bar exemplars plus the Andrew Tarvin safety filter.
