# Dry Bar Comedy Wave 2 Starter Theme Map

First-pass comparison map for the 37 available transcripts in the 40-video
starter slice.

## Main themes

### 1. Concrete premise before generalized lesson

Specific situations—bank robbery, parenting, diet, aging, work, body, or
relationship friction—give the listener a handle before interpretation.

### 2. Promise, setup, turn, payoff

The title or first premise creates an expectation; subsequent beats either
fulfil, complicate, or reverse it. The presentation adaptation is a truthful
promise followed by a delivered turn, not clickbait.

### 3. Contrast and frame replacement

The speaker lets one interpretation become available and then changes the
frame. This can clarify a presentation when the before/after distinction is
real and the speaker states the boundary.

### 4. Character specificity and point of view

The same everyday topic becomes distinct through who notices it, what they
want, and what detail they choose. This is useful for story selection and
example design, not for copying personality.

### 5. Room awareness and recovery

Direct address, heckle response, and acknowledgment are candidate delivery
functions. They must be reviewed with audio/video context because transcript
words alone cannot establish timing or audience response.

### 6. Self-inclusion as a possible safety boundary

Some difficult observations are framed through the speaker's own situation or
contradiction. In presentations, self-inclusion may reduce one-sidedness, but
it is not a universal safety rule and should be tested against topic and power
differences.

## Comparison use

Do not pool all 37 transcripts into one “comedy works” result. Compare premise
types, performer choices, format length, audience context, and missing-media
states separately. The slice is designed to generate contrast cases for the
existing ten-corpus system.
