# Starter Curriculum

First-pass self-training curriculum for the speaking research atlas.

Date: August 10, 2026.

## Purpose

The repo now has strong research and browsing layers, but a learner still
benefits from one compact answer to:

- What should I do this week if I want to get better, not just read?

This curriculum is a short first-pass sequence built from the strongest current
sessions, concepts, drills, and evidence moments.

It now also includes a concrete example layer for learners who understand the
idea only after seeing a real speaker or a before-and-after situation.

It should also now be read with the exact-approach layer in mind when the
learner wants the named transcript-backed move before opening the example
files.

For learners who want an even simpler entry point, use:

- `analysis/focused-improvement-actions.md`

## Who this curriculum is for

Best for learners who:

- want a practical starting point instead of exploring the whole atlas at once
- freeze, ramble, or sound flat under pressure
- want a balanced first pass across confidence, clarity, delivery, and
  audience connection

## Suggested weekly rhythm

Use this as a two-week cycle:

### Week 1

1. Sessions 1 to 3
2. Focus on confidence, anti-script fluency, and structure

### Week 2

1. Sessions 4 to 7
2. Focus on polish, openings, delivery, and connection

## Minimum viable repetition rule

If the learner only adopts one training rule from this curriculum:

- repeat each drill at least three times across separate days before deciding
  whether it works

That helps the repo stay aligned with one of its core principles:

- drills over inspiration
- repetition over one-off consumption

## Companion quick-action mode

If the full seven-session sequence feels too heavy, use the shorter practical
layer instead:

- pick one problem from `analysis/focused-improvement-actions.md`
- run one or two actions from that section for one week
- review what improved before adding more

If the drills still feel too abstract even after that:

- open `analysis/worked-speaking-situation-examples.md`
- or `analysis/real-speaker-exemplars.md`
- or `analysis/real-world-speaking-application-examples.md`
