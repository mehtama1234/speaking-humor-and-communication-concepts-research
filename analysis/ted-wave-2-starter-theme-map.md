# TED Wave 2 Starter Theme Map

First-pass comparison map for 38 available transcripts in the 40-talk starter slice.

## Main themes

### 1. Concrete opening before abstraction
Specific people, moments, and problems can create an audience handle before a general claim is introduced.

### 2. Question-to-thesis orientation
Questions can open an information gap, but the talk must eventually state what it is trying to explain or change.

### 3. Analogy and explanation bridges
Analogy, image, and concrete example are candidate bridges between technical content and listener understanding; reviewers must check where the analogy breaks.

### 4. Contrast and frame replacement
Before/after or apparent-versus-actual contrasts can make a change in interpretation visible without requiring a false binary.

### 5. Story to principle
Personal or observed narrative can earn attention, then become useful when the speaker explicitly marks the transferable principle and its limits.

### 6. Audience-oriented close
The close often returns to the audience with an implication, action, or memorable image. A transcript alone cannot establish whether the delivery landed.

## Exact-approach bridges to test
