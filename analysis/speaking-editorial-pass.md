## The product we are developing

Turn this repository's existing 27 speaking approaches and 11 curricula into a coherent, professionally written learning product. This is a separate body of material from the 55-pattern presentation fieldbook in best-speech-analysis. A cross-repository link does not constitute this editorial work.

Each approach needs a first-principles explanation, a closely read source example, an explicitly identified original application, context-sensitive alternatives, a failure case, and a practical exercise with observable review criteria. The curricula need developed teaching sequences and worked situations, not merely lists of links to these chapters.

Preserve the research notes and their provenance. Publish the reader-facing writing separately, then connect it to the existing site. Source commentary, an observed transcript passage, and our proposed teaching adaptation must remain distinguishable. A channel's promotional promise is not an established outcome; an AI-rewritten celebrity exchange is not something the celebrity actually said.

## Original completion criteria (now met; see release audit)

For completion, all 27 chapters and all 11 curricula must have substantive writing, not placeholders. Each chapter must be traceable to inspected source passages and name the limits of that inspection. Teaching examples must be identified as original rather than passed off as documentary evidence. Curricula must tell readers what to do, in what order, how to adapt it to their setting, and how to judge their work without pretending that an untested drill guarantees improvement.

The existing site provides a readable, navigable presentation of the completed writing. Links and page structure are checked, the content remains usable on small screens and without JavaScript, and a final editorial pass removes repetition, unsupported promises, contradictory advice and unnecessarily status-laden language. Research completion and editorial completion are reported separately.
