# TED Wave 2 Starter Drill Candidates

These are practice hypotheses derived from transcript retrieval, not validated drills.

1. Open with one concrete moment, then state the larger question in one sentence.
2. Explain a complex idea with one analogy, then name the analogy's limit.
3. Tell a short story and explicitly mark the principle it supports.
4. Build a before/after contrast without pretending the two frames are exhaustive.
5. End with a specific audience implication rather than a generic motivational line.

Each drill needs listener checks for clarity, recall, perceived relevance, and overclaiming before promotion.
