# Toastmasters International Wave 1 Theme Map

First-pass theme map for the `Toastmasters International` starter corpus.

Date: August 10, 2026.

If this theme map is being used to drive learner-facing extraction, pair it
with `analysis/transcript-backed-concrete-speaking-approaches.md`.

## Main themes

### 1. Strong speeches enter fast and orient the audience immediately

Representative sessions:

- `#44 2025 Toastmasters World Champion of Public Speaking`
- `#79 2023 Toastmasters World Champion of Public Speaking`
- `#95 2022 Toastmasters World Champion of Public Speaking`
- `#111 World Championship of Public Speaking®: Speaker Showcase`

What this theme appears to govern:

- first lines
- opening scenes
- early tension
- immediate audience orientation

### 2. Storytelling works when specificity carries universal meaning

Representative sessions:

- `#65 Connect and Inspire Through Storytelling`
- `#109 A Conversation Between Two World Champions`
- `#77 The 2023 World Champion ... Shares the Makings of Her Story`
- `#94 The 2022 World Champion ... Shares the Makings of His Story`

What this theme appears to govern:

- story selection
- scene choice
- emotional truth
- connecting personal material to shared human meaning

### 3. Humor is a craft of observation, fit, and timing

Representative sessions:

- `#128 The Humor Workshop—Part I`
- `#127 The Humor Workshop—Part II`
- `#109 A Conversation Between Two World Champions`

What this theme appears to govern:

- humor notebooks
- callbacks
- self-deprecating humor
- crowd reading
- not stepping on laughter

### 4. Delivery is physical, not just verbal

Representative sessions:

- `#88 How to Use Gestures and Body Language to Keep Your Audience Engaged`
- `#127 The Humor Workshop—Part II`
- `#128 The Humor Workshop—Part I`

What this theme appears to govern:

- gesture use
- facial expression
- physical humor
- arrival energy
- using the body to support meaning

### 5. Audience adaptation is part of the speech, not post-processing

Representative sessions:

- `#111 World Championship of Public Speaking®: Speaker Showcase`
- `#109 A Conversation Between Two World Champions`
- `#127 The Humor Workshop—Part II`

What this theme appears to govern:

- room reading
- audience-specific phrasing
- international audience adaptation
- vulnerability calibration
- revising stories from the listener’s point of view

### 6. Impromptu speaking becomes manageable when structure is borrowed

Representative sessions:

- `#87 Impromptu Speaking: How to Get Better at Speaking on the Spot`
- `#86 How to Give a Memorable Toast`
- `#90 5 Public Speaking Tips to Speak with Confidence`

What this theme appears to govern:

- speaking on short notice
- occasion speaking
- anti-rambling structure
- staying coherent under surprise
