## Theme 1: Flow over memorization

Core idea:
Strong speaking is treated as adaptive navigation, not script recital.

Key sessions:

- `#4 How to Speak Brilliantly Well – Without a Script (Ali Abdaal)`
- `#10 How to Speak like a CEO`
- `#11 Impress Mode vs. Express Mode`

Subthemes:

- talking points over full scripting
- internalization over memorization
- expressive presence over presentational stiffness
