# Live Field Missions

Concrete real-world speaking assignments for taking one move out of practice
and into an actual room this week.

Date: August 10, 2026.

## Purpose

The repo already has:

- drills
- examples
- roleplays
- weekly sprints
- practice recipes
- partner-run rehearsal

What it still needed was an even simpler transfer layer:

- what exactly should I go do in real life this week?

This file turns practice into fieldwork.

## How to use this file

1. Pick one mission only.
2. Before choosing the mission wording, open
   `analysis/transcript-backed-concrete-speaking-approaches.md` and pick the
   exact move you are trying to protect in the room.
2. Run it in a real situation within seven days.
3. Do not stack three missions at once.
4. Write down:
   - where you used it
   - whether you actually did the move
   - what changed in the room
   - what broke

## Mission 1: Give one clearer project update

Use when:

- work updates sound cluttered
- you bury the point under context

What to do:

- in one real update this week, say only:
  - current status
  - main risk
  - next step or recommendation

Success looks like:

- the room understands the update faster
- fewer clarifying questions arrive
- your recommendation is audible early

## Mission 3: Ask one depth-creating follow-up

Use when:

- your conversations stay surface-level
- you switch back to yourself too quickly

What to do:

- in one real conversation this week, ask one follow-up that targets:
  - turning point
  - difficulty
  - surprise
  - meaning

Examples:

- `What made that harder than it looked from the outside?`
- `What was the turning point?`
- `What surprised you most?`

Success looks like:

- the other person expands more
- the conversation gets less generic
- you feel less pressure to perform
