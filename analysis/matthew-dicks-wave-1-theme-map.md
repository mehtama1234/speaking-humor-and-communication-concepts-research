# Matthew Dicks Wave 1 Theme Map

First-pass theme map for the `Matthew Dicks` starter corpus.

Date: August 10, 2026.

If this theme map is being used to drive learner-facing extraction, pair it
with `analysis/transcript-backed-concrete-speaking-approaches.md`.

## Main themes

### 1. Story worthiness before story polish

Representative sessions:

- `#9 The Story Problem Nobody Talks About`
- `#25 How to Know If a Story Is Worth Telling`
- `#42 The Story That Won: Trader Joe's, Masks, and One Perfect Moment`

What this theme appears to govern:

- whether a moment deserves expansion into a story
- what kinds of moments create attention
- why many speakers pick moments that feel meaningful to them but land flat
  for listeners

### 2. Openings should create movement, not fake suspense

Representative sessions:

- `#4 Stop Using Story Hooks. Do This in the First 30s.`
- `#46 Why Speeches Are Forgotten Without Story`
- `#48 Why Attention Is Earned Through Story`

What this theme appears to govern:

- the first 30 seconds of a story or speech
- why teaser-style hooks often underperform
- how to move listeners into narrative quickly

### 3. Scene and specificity create attention

Representative sessions:

- `#13 The Storytelling Choice That Made This Ad Work`
- `#47 The Mistake That Breaks a Story`
- `#48 Why Attention Is Earned Through Story`

What this theme appears to govern:

- the difference between explaining and showing
- why detail selection matters
- what breaks narrative energy

### 4. Story is a memory and persuasion tool for speakers

Representative sessions:

- `#46 Why Speeches Are Forgotten Without Story`
- `#44 A Masterclass in Storytelling`
- `#40 Why My Stories Win at The Moth`

What this theme appears to govern:

- why stories make speeches stick
- how narrative changes audience attention and recall
- why ideas alone are easier to forget than lived movement

### 5. Exemplars are part of the teaching system

Representative sessions:

- `#11 Charity Thief`
- `#15 Stranger in Panera`
- `#31 The Robbery`
- `#50 The Story That Won The Moth GrandSLAM`

What this theme appears to govern:

- translating advice into real story study
- reverse-engineering winning performance choices
- building stronger exemplar-backed speaking notes

## Strongest exact-approach bridge

This corpus most clearly appears to deepen:

- `Approach 18`

Likely sub-moves inside that approach:

- choose a moment worth telling
- open with live movement instead of generic framing
- use scene and specificity to carry attention
- let the lesson arrive after the audience is already invested
