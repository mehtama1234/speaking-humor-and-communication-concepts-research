# Communication Coach Alexander Lyon Wave 1 Theme Map

First-pass theme map for the `Communication Coach Alexander Lyon` starter
corpus.

Date: August 10, 2026.

If this theme map is being used to drive learner-facing extraction, pair it
with `analysis/transcript-backed-concrete-speaking-approaches.md`.

## Main themes

### 1. Plain language creates clarity and confidence

Representative sessions:

- `#78 How to Communicate Clearly and Concisely (Free Mini-Training)`
- `#353 Concise Sentences`
- `#354 Concise Message`
- `#355 Long-windedness`

What this theme appears to govern:

- reducing verbal clutter
- replacing fancy language with clear language
- trimming long-winded explanations
- making ideas easier to follow

### 2. Executive presence is composure plus structure

Representative sessions:

- `#46 How to Develop Executive Presence`
- `#54 How to Communicate Like a CEO`
- `#79 How to Speak with Conviction`
- `#87 How to be Seen as the Leader in the Room`

What this theme appears to govern:

- gravitas
- control over expression
- sounding composed rather than scattered
- leading with structure and weight

### 3. Strong workplace communication starts with framing

Representative sessions:

- `#84 How to Get to the Point when Speaking`
- `#54 How to Communicate Like a CEO`
- `#267 Clear Communication in the Workplace`

What this theme appears to govern:

- telling listeners what the topic is
- making the intent clear early
- helping people know what to do with the information
- moving into detail only after the frame is set

### 4. Credibility under pressure comes from honest steadiness

Representative sessions:

- `#67 How To Say "I don't know" With Credibility!`
- `#79 How to Speak with Conviction`
- `#111 Classy Communication Habits Top 10`

What this theme appears to govern:

- uncertainty handling
- conviction without bluffing
- staying calm in Q&A
- sounding professional without overacting certainty

### 5. Leadership communication is built from daily relational habits

Representative sessions:

- `#90 Positive Communication Skills for Leaders (complete series)`
- `#91 Effective Listening Skills for Leaders`
- `#118 Reflective Listening Skills`

What this theme appears to govern:

- greeting
- asking questions
- complimenting and encouraging
- disclosure and relationship building
- listening to strengthen trust

### 6. Meetings improve when expectations are explicit

Representative sessions:

- `#113 How to Run an Effective Meeting 5 Tips`
- `#299 Upward Communication: Workplace Communication Skills`
- `#300 Workplace Communication Skills: Monologue or Dialogue?`
- `#348 Effective Communication Skills When You're New to a Group or Team`

What this theme appears to govern:

- agenda discipline
- expected contributions
- dialogue over one-way information dumping
- role clarity in team settings
