## Drill 1: Goal-audience-data planning card

Purpose:
Force every communication task to become audience-aware before delivery begins.

Likely source sessions:

- `#14 Know Before You Tell - The Three-Part Framework Every Communicator Needs`
- `#16 Don't Scare, Inspire: How to Talk About AI Without Losing Your Audience`

How to run it:

1. Before speaking, write three lines:
   - goal
   - audience
   - what evidence or feedback matters
2. Deliver the message only after all three are clear.

What it appears to build:

- intentionality
- audience focus
- message discipline

Best exact approaches:

- `Approach 5: Prepare With Goal, Audience, And Constraints`

## Drill 2: First-five-seconds opening reps

Purpose:
Improve the first moment after attention suddenly turns toward you.

Likely source sessions:

- `#13 The First 5 Seconds: How to Start a Speech the Moment All Eyes Suddenly Turn to You | AMA`
- `#15 Quick Thinks: How to Look Confident, Composed, and Credible`

How to run it:

1. Simulate sudden speaking prompts.
2. Deliver only the first ten to twenty seconds.
3. Repeat until your opening sounds clear, calm, and intentional.

What it appears to build:

- composure
- immediate clarity
- strong entry behavior

Best exact approaches:

- `Approach 4: Use The First Five Seconds To Establish Direction`
- `Approach 21: Use Unhurried Pace To Claim Space`

## Drill 3: PREP argument reps

Purpose:
Train structured answers when making a claim or defending a position.

Likely source sessions:

- `#30 Ask Matt Anything: Authenticity, Anxiety, and Answering Well`

How to run it:

1. Pick a question that requires a point of view.
2. Answer in four moves:
   - point
   - reason
   - example
   - point
3. Practice until the structure becomes natural.

What it appears to build:

- answer organization
- argument clarity
- persuasion under pressure

Best exact approaches:

- `Approach 19: Take A Position, Give A Reason, Then Reopen`

## Drill 4: Technical explanation rewrite

Purpose:
Make dense or technical material easier to absorb.

Likely source sessions:

- `#2 Don't Lose the Room: How to Make Ideas Stick`
- `#16 Don't Scare, Inspire: How to Talk About AI Without Losing Your Audience`
- `#1 Your Voice Is a Wind Instrument | Matt Abrahams on Projecting Without Straining`

How to run it:

1. Take a technical explanation you already use.
2. Rewrite it for a non-specialist audience.
3. Deliver it aloud and remove any phrasing that causes listener lag.

What it appears to build:

- audience-centered clarity
- stickiness
- verbal accessibility

Best exact approaches:

- `Approach 14: Simplify Complex Ideas By Layering`

## Drill 5: Humor as connection rep

Purpose:
Use light humor or levity to create warmth without forcing stand-up energy.

Likely source sessions:

- `#8 The Science of Funny: How Humor Builds Connection`
- `#22 Hello, Stranger: Why Curiosity Beats Charisma Every Time`

How to run it:

1. Retell a recent minor frustration, surprise, or odd moment.
2. Focus on human relatability rather than punchline perfection.
3. Notice whether the telling builds connection and ease.

What it appears to build:

- warmth
- relatability
- low-pressure humor

Best exact approaches:

- `Approach 11: Use Humor As Shared Relief, Not Performance`
- `Approach 17: Put The Turn Late And Leave Room For The Laugh`

## Drill 6: Curiosity-first conversation swap

Purpose:
Reduce self-performance pressure by shifting into question-led engagement.

Likely source sessions:

- `#22 Hello, Stranger: Why Curiosity Beats Charisma Every Time`

How to run it:

1. In a live conversation, aim to ask two genuinely interested follow-up questions before trying to impress.
2. Track whether the interaction becomes easier and more fluid.

What it appears to build:

- connection
- listening
- lower self-consciousness

Best exact approaches:

- `Approach 8: Use Curiosity To Create Better Conversations`
- `Approach 16: Start With Connection Before Content`

## Drill 7: Clarity-over-perfection voice rep

Purpose:
Reduce accent and perfection anxiety by optimizing for understandability.

Likely source sessions:

- `#6 Accent-uate, Don't Compensate: Why Clarity Matters More Than Perfection`
- `#29 Stop Fixing Your Accent: What Linguists Know That Language Teachers Don't`

How to run it:

1. Record a short explanation.
2. Ask listeners only one question: “Was this easy to understand?”
3. Improve intelligibility, not accent conformity.

What it appears to build:

- relaxed clarity
- anti-perfectionism
- accessible delivery

Best exact approaches:

- `Approach 23: Improve Intelligibility Without Accent Erasure`
