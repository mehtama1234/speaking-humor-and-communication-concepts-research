# Presentation And Formal Speaking Curriculum

Focused self-training sequence for talks, demos, presentations, and other
high-attention speaking moments.

Date: August 10, 2026.

## Purpose

The atlas already helps with:

- general starter training
- business communication
- social speaking and humor

But many learners have a more specific practical need:

- I need to sound stronger when all eyes are on me

This file turns the current presentation-facing layers of the atlas into a
compact ordered route instead of leaving them scattered across concepts,
pathways, and session notes.

## Who this curriculum is for

Best for learners who:

- become stiff or over-scripted in talks
- lose the room with weak openings
- sound flatter in presentations than in conversation
- need stronger demos, presentations, or formal spoken updates

## Suggested two-week rhythm

### Week 1

1. Steps 1 to 3
2. Focus on scriptlessness, openings, and structure

### Week 2

1. Steps 4 to 6
2. Focus on delivery, polish, and memorability

## Minimum viable presentation rule

If the learner only adopts one rule from this curriculum:

- rehearse the opening, the structure, and the close, not every sentence

That one shift reduces:

- stiffness
- panic after losing a line
- flatter delivery
- weak audience connection
