# Source Map

Structured first-pass source map for the speaking, humor, and communication
research workspace.

Captured on August 9, 2026.

## Tier 1: Must-ingest framework channels

These are the best first channels to ingest because they directly teach
speaking improvement methods.

### Ultraspeaking

- url: `https://www.youtube.com/c/ultraspeaking`
- role:
  - speaking confidence
  - flow
  - spontaneity
  - anti-script speaking
  - drills
- why first:
  - closest fit to the repo's practical improvement goal
  - likely to yield reusable speaking exercises and concept language quickly

### Vinh Giang

- url: `https://www.youtube.com/@askvinh`
- role:
  - delivery
  - vocal variety
  - stage presence
  - energy
  - audience engagement
- why first:
  - unusually strong on turning delivery into something concrete and teachable
  - should produce a rich concept layer around presence and performance

### Think Fast Talk Smart

- url: `https://www.youtube.com/@ThinkFastTalkSmart`
- role:
  - structured communication
  - thinking under pressure
  - speaking anxiety
  - leadership and workplace communication
- why first:
  - strong complement to the more performance-oriented channels
  - useful for practical speaking in meetings, interviews, and work settings

## Tier 3: Example libraries for speech study

These channels matter because they provide real examples to analyze.

### Toastmasters International

- url: `https://www.youtube.com/toastmasters`
- role:
  - speech examples
  - competition speeches
  - openings
  - speech craft
- research value:
  - useful for seeing what successful public-speaking structure sounds like

### TED

- url: `https://www.youtube.com/ted`
- role:
  - polished examples
  - storytelling
  - persuasion
  - memorable phrasing
- research value:
  - strong source for studying talk architecture and high-end delivery

### TEDx Talks

- url: `https://www.youtube.com/user/TEDxTalks`
- role:
  - broad example corpus
  - stronger and weaker talk comparisons
  - hooks
  - energy management
- research value:
  - useful because the quality variance makes comparison easier

## Tier 4: Social charisma and conversational presence

These are useful because strong speaking is not only formal speech.

### Charisma on Command

- url: `https://www.youtube.com/user/charismaoncommand`
- role:
  - conversational charisma
  - likability
  - humor
  - social confidence
- research value:
  - helps extend the repo beyond stage speaking into live human interaction

## Tier 5: Still missing and should be added deliberately

These source categories are not yet well-covered.

- humor-first channels that teach comedic timing or joke mechanics
- storytelling-first channels that explain narrative structure in spoken delivery
- improv-based speaking sources
- interview and podcast-style exemplars of strong spontaneous speaking
- channels focused on speaking for founders, sales, or persuasion under stakes

## Recommended ingestion order

1. Ultraspeaking
2. Vinh Giang
3. Think Fast Talk Smart
4. Toastmasters International
5. Charisma on Command

This order gives the repo:

- drills
- delivery frameworks
- structured communication frameworks
- speech examples
- social charisma patterns
