# Stand-Up Studios Wave 3 Starter Drill Candidates

No transcript-derived drills are promoted. The 1/22 transcript coverage is too sparse to distinguish a teachable speaking choice from a title-level promise or a performer-specific act.

Deferred practice hypotheses:

1. Compare a spoof frame with a literal frame and inspect whether the audience can identify the intended frame.
2. Compare a showcase clip's setup, turn, pause, and payoff after synchronized media review.
3. Test whether a “make boring fun” move transfers to an ordinary presentation without copying a stand-up persona.
