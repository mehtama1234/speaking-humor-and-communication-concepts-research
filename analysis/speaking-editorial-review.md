## Review standard

For each approach, inspect whether the principle explains a mechanism rather than only naming a tip; the source reading and invented application are distinguished; the application demonstrates the specific move; the exercise produces observable feedback; limits do not contradict the recommendation; and related chapters do different jobs. Review source accuracy against the already inspected passages when a change affects attribution or a claim. A prose-review entry alone is not a new complete-video review.

For each learning path, inspect continuity of the worked case, progression of the sessions, concrete outputs, adaptation to another setting, and criteria for deciding what to practice next. Timings must remain suggestions, not outcome guarantees. Fictional cases must remain clearly identified.

## Book-level editorial disposition

This assessment draws on the complete individual readings recorded above, plus the reader orientation, evidence note and the numbered original inventory. It is a comparison across the authored product, not a new full-corpus analysis.

- **Teaching distinctions:** compression selects information; layering orders dependencies; navigation preserves a route; pacing allocates time. Direction, relevance and conversational intent answer different questions. Curiosity follows offered detail; reflection checks interpretation; warmth establishes participation; repair addresses a specific problem. Objection preparation, responding under pressure, reopening evaluation and asking for a stage-appropriate decision are treated separately. Humor choice precedes humor construction. PREP finishes an answer while PRO invites discussion. The chapters state these distinctions and cross-link rather than requiring a learner to infer them from labels alone.
- **Example diversity:** the editorial changes to chapters 01, 02, 10 and 21 reduce repeated launch/pilot material, introducing peer feedback, a newsletter date, a recording/caption decision and conference programming. Retained workplace cases have different jobs: numerical compression, capacity uncertainty, training workload, approval flow, ownership, and evaluation versus implementation. The paths add photography, exhibition labels, equipment lending, a reading group, a hosted event, an interview, a photo handoff, library orientation, induction design, a puzzle swap and a family album. Shared workplace vocabulary is not by itself needless duplication.
- **Voice and proportion:** principles begin with recognizable situations and proceed to explanations, contrasting examples and actions. Practical judgments replace promises of status, universal persuasion or a prescribed professional voice. Corrections made during review include authority boundaries, ambiguous timing, causal overstatement, unsupported delivery interpretations and manufactured partner corrections. These are substantive changes, not simply softer wording.
- **Repeated evidence language:** retain the short invented-case labels and per-chapter inspection scopes. Readers can enter through any chapter, so moving all distinctions into a single preface would make excerpts easier to mistake for real cases. The orientation and evidence note explain the shared rules once; local source discussions retain only distinctions material to that source, such as scripted scenes, reported anecdotes, transcript-only delivery evidence or AI-proposed replies. This deliberate repetition is preferable to losing provenance at the point of use.
- **Progression and outputs:** paths reuse principles but not the full chapter explanations. Their continuing cases develop session outputs and conclude with a complete task, a listener check or a transfer attempt. Suggested timings remain estimates. Feedback evaluates the message and the next action, not the learner's identity or another person's compelled reaction.
- **Relationship to the research:** numbering preserves all 27 inventory slots. Reframing status, interruption, humor and confidence language is explained in the relevant chapter/path source notes rather than silently attributed to the source speaker. The authored selection's 30 cited videos are not represented as complete analysis of every video in the wider research repository.

Disposition: the book-level editorial pass is complete for this manuscript. No unresolved contradiction was identified in these reviewed teaching sequences after the recorded corrections. This is an editorial judgment, not a guarantee of learner outcomes, an external peer review or publication sign-off. Final status still requires the artifact and requirement audit.
