# TED Wave 2 Starter Corpus Brief

This is a bounded comparison slice, not a claim about all TED talks. The channel manifest contains 4,842 videos; the starter manifest samples 40 title-qualified talks from distinct speaker keys. Transcript retrieval produced 38 available transcripts, 81,429 words, and 15,008 cues. Two selected talks were unavailable during retrieval: `4WUVkpC8MKs` and `nLw3o-XgEdo`.

The slice is useful for comparing polished-talk architecture with the existing humor and communication corpora: concrete openings, question-to-thesis orientation, analogy, contrast, story-to-principle transitions, and audience-directed closes. It does not establish that these patterns cause attention, trust, learning, or behavior change. Those claims require contextual review, delivery evidence, independent judgment, transfer testing, and listener outcomes.
