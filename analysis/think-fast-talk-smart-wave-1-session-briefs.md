# Think Fast Talk Smart Wave 1 Session Briefs

Short working briefs for the first `Think Fast Talk Smart` transcript corpus.

Date: August 10, 2026.

If these briefs are being used to pick learner-facing extraction targets, pair
them with `analysis/transcript-backed-concrete-speaking-approaches.md`.

## Session briefs

### 1. Your Voice Is a Wind Instrument | Matt Abrahams on Projecting Without Straining

- Voice-focused session on projection without brute-force vocal effort.
- Best used for the repo’s clarity, projection, and physical voice-use concepts.

### 2. Don't Lose the Room: How to Make Ideas Stick

- Strong audience-retention and message-stickiness session.
- Good anchor for making complex ideas absorbable without losing momentum.
  Best exact approaches:
  - `Approach 14`

### 4. Stretch, Don't Stress: How Great Communication Makes Better Leaders

- Connects communication to leadership function rather than presentation polish alone.
- Useful for leadership communication and culture-shaping tracks.

### 5. Words Under Fire: Why Speaking A Second Language Gets Harder In High-Stakes Talks: Ask Matt Anything

- Expands the repo into multilingual pressure communication.
- Helpful for understanding that stress and clarity are tightly coupled.

### 6. Accent-uate, Don't Compensate: Why Clarity Matters More Than Perfection

- Pushes against perfectionist language standards and toward audience comprehension.
- Strong source for a clarity-over-perfection concept page.
  Best exact approaches:
  - `Approach 23`

### 8. The Science of Funny: How Humor Builds Connection

- One of the best humor sessions in the current source base.
- Treats humor as relational and humanizing, not only performance-based.
  Best exact approaches:
  - `Approach 11`
  - `Approach 17`

### 9. How to Pitch Anything: 10 20 30 Rule by Guy Kawasaki

- Pitch-structure session with obvious workplace and founder relevance.
- Useful for concise persuasion and deck-driven communication.

### 13. The First 5 Seconds: How to Start a Speech the Moment All Eyes Suddenly Turn to You | AMA

- High-value session on openings and being put on the spot.
- Strong anchor for first-moment composure and start-strong frameworks.
  Best exact approaches:
  - `Approach 4`
  - `Approach 21`

### 14. Know Before You Tell - The Three-Part Framework Every Communicator Needs

- One of the strongest structure-first sessions in the slice.
- Emphasizes goal, audience, and feedback/data style thinking.
  Best exact approaches:
  - `Approach 5`
  - `Approach 14`

### 15. Quick Thinks: How to Look Confident, Composed, and Credible

- Compact session on visible calm credibility.
- Best tied to workplace presence and high-stakes speaking.

### 16. Don't Scare, Inspire: How to Talk About AI Without Losing Your Audience

- Technical-topic communication session that emphasizes audience experience.
- Very useful for speaking about difficult or overloaded subjects.

### 19. Stanford's Top Negotiator Reveals What Most People Get Dead Wrong

- Negotiation-oriented communication session.
- Likely valuable for disagreement, framing, and conversation under conflict.

### 21. Headspace Habits: Lessons for Calm, Confident Communication

- Communication through mental state and self-regulation rather than only tactics.
- Good support for calm-confidence learner paths.

### 22. Hello, Stranger: Why Curiosity Beats Charisma Every Time

- Important corrective to charisma-centered advice.
- Makes curiosity itself a communicative superpower.
  Best exact approaches:
  - `Approach 8`
  - `Approach 16`

### 29. Stop Fixing Your Accent: What Linguists Know That Language Teachers Don't

- Strong anti-perfection and pro-understandability session.
- Useful for accent, inclusion, and clarity concepts.

### 30. Ask Matt Anything: Authenticity, Anxiety, and Answering Well

- Broad Q&A session that combines anxiety, response quality, and visible credibility.
- Good source for structure-based answering advice.

### 34. Trial Lawyer’s Communication Advice: DON’T Try to WIN Arguments!

- Conflict and disagreement session that likely centers persuasion over domination.
- Good fit for argument, debate, and hard-conversation concept work.
