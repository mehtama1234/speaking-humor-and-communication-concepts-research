Unified End-to-End System

Source material becomes a practice path.

This page connects the channel atlas to the stricter best-speech-analysis evidence and validation layer. The goal is traceability from a real speaking problem to exact source context, a reusable move, practice, presentation adaptation, and a listener check.

Evidence boundary

Transcript-derived candidates are useful for discovery and practice design. They are not silently promoted to independently validated or effective techniques.

The pipeline

One learner journey

01 · Source

Channel and transcript

Choose a source, video, context, transcript segment, and timestamp.

02 · Meaning

Job and pattern

Identify what the speaker is doing and map it to a shared communication job.

03 · Practice

Drill and adaptation

Practise the underlying choice, add a non-fit boundary, and adapt it to a presentation or real room.

04 · Evidence

Review and recurrence

Compare speakers and events while preserving denominators, missingness, and uncertainty.

05 · Outcome

Learner and listener check

Test recognition, transfer, revision, comprehension, or recall separately from popularity.

06 · Release

Promotion decision

Promote only when the provenance, review, counterexample, safety, and outcome gates support it.
