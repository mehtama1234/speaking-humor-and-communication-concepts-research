Techniques&nbsp;
Now that we've pinpointed a few&nbsp;
potential places of practice,&nbsp;&nbsp;
let's take a closer look at the techniques&nbsp;
to help you manage your impromptu speaking.
The first approach is a simple formula&nbsp;
known as PREP, which stands for: Point,&nbsp;&nbsp;
Reason, Example, Point.
Start your speech by&nbsp;&nbsp;
making a clear point about a subject. For&nbsp;
example, Internet outages are annoying.&nbsp;
Next, explain why you are speaking on the&nbsp;
topic, then give a few examples to bring&nbsp;&nbsp;
life to your story, and, finally, end with a&nbsp;
concluding point that wraps up your speech.&nbsp;
Those might include things like,&nbsp;
I m speaking on this topic because&nbsp;&nbsp;
it happened today and that is why I am at the&nbsp;
market, waiting in this long line. After that,&nbsp;&nbsp;
you might add another anecdote[A8] and finally,&nbsp;
a conclusion about internet unreliability.&nbsp;
By following this flow, you'll be able&nbsp;
to clearly state your main point and help&nbsp;&nbsp;
keep your ideas on course.
PREP is an excellent fit&nbsp;&nbsp;
for more professional settings such as&nbsp;
business meetings and even interviews.
Another way to manage your speaking&nbsp;
time is by following the pros vs. cons,&nbsp;&nbsp;
or positive and negative method.
Set up an issue by addressing the&nbsp;&nbsp;
pros first, the cons second, and then neatly&nbsp;
wrap up your point with your recommendation.&nbsp;&nbsp;
Pros vs. cons also works well in a professional&nbsp;
setting and in many every-day settings as well.&nbsp;
Filling in for a speaker
If you find yourself filling in&nbsp;&nbsp;
for a speaker, quickly think about how you&nbsp;
would open and close your speech. You may&nbsp;&nbsp;
begin by sharing a few personal stories, and then&nbsp;
engaging the audience by acting as a moderator.&nbsp;&nbsp;
You can encourage questions and answers&nbsp;
to extend the speaking time if needed.&nbsp;
Now that you've considered how&nbsp;
to prepare for your speech,&nbsp;&nbsp;
it's time to concentrate on the final and most&nbsp;
important step: delivering your impromptu speech.&nbsp;&nbsp;
The only way to master impromptu speaking is&nbsp;
by jumping in and practicing in real-time.&nbsp;&nbsp;
If you struggle with being a perfectionist,&nbsp;
don't stress. Simply try to have fun. Most&nbsp;&nbsp;
people at the event will applaud your&nbsp;
courage and willingness to stand in.&nbsp;
